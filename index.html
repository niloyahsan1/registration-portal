Workshop Details

BRAC University Film Club is hosting an exclusive Scriptwriting Workshop featuring Ayman Asib Shadhin, renowned scriptwriter of Utshob, Kaiser, and Mainkar Chipay. The session is designed to enhance students' understanding of storytelling, screenplay structure, and creative writing for film and television.

This workshop is open to all university students interested in film, literature, or creative writing.

🎯 Objectives

  • To introduce participants to the fundamentals of professional scriptwriting.
  • To explore the creative process behind acclaimed Bangladeshi productions.
  • To encourage student engagement in creative writing and film development.

📑 Session Outline

  • Comprehensive discussion on developing a script from concept to final draft.
  • Exploration of different genres and narrative structures.
  • Interactive Q&A session with the guest speaker.
